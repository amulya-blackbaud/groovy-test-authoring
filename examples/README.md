# Examples

Drop one or two **real, exemplary** test files from your codebase here (or link
to them by path from the per-type reference files). Suggested:

- `CoreTestExample.groovy`
- `ComponentTestExample.groovy`
- `SharedTestExample.groovy`

A live example that already follows your conventions is the most powerful thing
in this skill — Claude will mirror its structure, imports, naming, and setup far
more reliably than it will follow a prose description. Prefer copying a real test
verbatim over writing a synthetic one.

Keep these current: if your conventions change, update the examples too, or they
become misleading.
